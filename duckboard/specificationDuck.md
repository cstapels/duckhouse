# Duck House Controller — Hardware Specification

**Revision:** 1.7
**Purpose:** ESP32-based controller for automated duck-house lift/door actuators, manual controls, environmental sensing, and battery monitoring.

---

## 1. System Overview

The controller operates from a nominal **12 V lead-acid battery** and controls two 12 V linear actuators:

1. **Lift actuator**
2. **Door actuator**

Each actuator is controlled by an external **BTS7960 / IBT-2 motor controller**. Motor current does **not** pass through the PCB.

The PCB contains:

* ESP32U DevKit
* 12 V battery input and PCB protection
* External 5 V buck-converter interface
* Switched 5 V logic supply for the BTS7960 modules
* Four manual pushbuttons
* Three external limit switches
* Analog photosensor input
* Two travel-time adjustment potentiometers
* Battery-voltage measurement
* Status LED
* Connectors for all external devices

The ESP32's onboard regulator supplies the PCB's 3.3 V rail from the 5 V supply.

# 2. Battery Input and Power

### J1 — Battery Input

2-pin, approximately 5.08 mm / 0.2" terminal block.

| Pin | Signal |
| --- | ------ |
| 1   | VBAT+  |
| 2   | GND    |

Battery range:

* Nominal: 12 V
* Expected operating range: approximately 10.5–14.5 V

### PCB Protection

Battery input sequence:

**J1 → 8 A fuse → reverse-polarity protection → VBAT**

The 8 A fuse protects the **PCB/controller branch only**.

The actuator motor power branches are separately fused and bypass the PCB.

### Reverse Polarity

Use an N-channel MOSFET reverse-polarity protection arrangement.

Exact MOSFET/controller implementation remains TBD.

### PCB Filtering

Provide:

* Bulk electrolytic capacitor near battery input
* Ceramic high-frequency bypass capacitor
* Optional TVS protection, value TBD

## BTS7960 Logic Power

| GPIO   | Function                   |
| ------ | -------------------------- |
| GPIO17 | BTS7960 logic power switch |

GPIO17 controls a discrete high-side 5 V switch.

### High-Side Switch

**27P04 P-channel MOSFET**

* Source → +5 V
* Drain → `MOTOR_CTRL_5V`
* Gate → 30N06L drain

**30N06L N-channel MOSFET**

* Drain → 27P04 gate
* Source → GND
* Gate → GPIO17

Resistors:

* 100 kΩ from 27P04 gate to +5 V
* 10 kΩ from 30N06L gate to GND

Operation:

* GPIO17 LOW/floating → BTS7960 logic power OFF
* GPIO17 HIGH → BTS7960 logic power ON

The motor controllers' **12 V motor supply remains permanently connected to the external battery/motor-power wiring**.

This switch controls only the BTS7960 **logic supply**.

# 6. ESP32 Boot/Strapping Pin Protection

Several application GPIOs are also ESP32 strapping pins. The PCB provides explicit external pulldowns so these signals have a predictable inactive state during reset and boot.

### GPIO2 — Limit Switch Power

GPIO2 supplies power to the limit-switch circuits.

Add:

**10 kΩ from GPIO2 to GND**

Boot behavior:

* ESP32 reset/boot → GPIO2 LOW
* Firmware initialization → GPIO2 configured as output
* After initialization → GPIO2 driven HIGH when limit switches are being read

The limit switches therefore do not need to be powered during ESP32 startup.

### GPIO5 — Door BTS7960 Enable

Add:

**10 kΩ from GPIO5 to GND**

This keeps the Door Enable signal LOW during reset/boot.

The BTS7960 logic supply is separately controlled by GPIO17 and remains OFF during startup.

### GPIO12 — Lift BTS7960 LPWM

Add:

**10 kΩ from GPIO12 to GND**

This provides an explicit LOW state during reset/boot.

The BTS7960 logic supply is OFF during startup, so the motor controller cannot respond to the PWM signal during boot.

### GPIO15 — Door Open Limit

The existing limit-switch input circuit already provides:

**10 kΩ from GPIO15 to GND**

This remains in place.

### GPIO17 — BTS7960 Logic Power

The existing motor-logic power switch already provides:

**10 kΩ from GPIO17 to GND**

This guarantees that the 30N06L remains OFF while the ESP32 is resetting or booting.

### Boot-State Resistor Summary

| GPIO   | Function            | External pulldown |
| ------ | ------------------- | ----------------: |
| GPIO2  | Limit-switch power  |             10 kΩ |
| GPIO5  | Door BTS7960 enable |             10 kΩ |
| GPIO12 | Lift LPWM           |             10 kΩ |
| GPIO15 | Door Open limit     |             10 kΩ |
| GPIO17 | BTS7960 logic power |             10 kΩ |

GPIO0 is not used by the application and the ESP32 DevKit's existing BOOT circuitry should be retained.

### Recommended Firmware Startup Sequence

The firmware should establish safe output states before enabling motor-controller logic:

1. ESP32 resets
2. GPIO2 remains LOW
3. GPIO5 remains LOW
4. GPIO12 remains LOW
5. GPIO14 remains LOW
6. GPIO16 remains LOW
7. GPIO19 remains LOW
8. GPIO13 remains LOW
9. GPIO17 remains LOW
10. Configure GPIO directions and safe states
11. Initialize inputs/ADC
12. Start required communications
13. Drive GPIO17 HIGH to enable BTS7960 logic
14. Enable motor control only when an intentional command is present

This ensures the motor controllers cannot receive an unintended command during ESP32 startup.

# 9. Actuator Power

Two approximately 12 V linear actuators are used.

Current design target:

* Approximately 5 A specified operating current
* Approximately 6 A design maximum
* Exact startup/stall current not yet established

Each actuator receives power through its own external BTS7960.

Conceptually:

**Battery → external fuse → BTS7960 → actuator**

There will be **no high-current actuator traces or motor connectors on the controller PCB**.

Final external fuse sizes should be selected after measuring actual actuator startup/stall current and considering wire size.

---

# 10. Limit Switches

Three external limit switches are required:

1. Door Open
2. Door Close
3. Lift Close

There is no Lift Open limit switch in the current design.

### Common Limit-Switch Power

GPIO2 supplies the limit-switch circuits.

GPIO2 is driven HIGH by firmware when the limit switches are being read.

### Limit GPIO Assignment

| GPIO   | Function   |
| ------ | ---------- |
| GPIO15 | Door Open  |
| GPIO21 | Door Close |
| GPIO25 | Lift Close |

### Input Circuit

Each limit circuit uses:

**GPIO2 → 470 Ω → switch wiring → limit switch → 470 Ω → sense GPIO**

Each sense input has:

* 10 kΩ pulldown to GND
* 100 nF capacitor from GPIO to GND

Expected operation:

* Switch open → LOW
* Switch closed → HIGH

---

# 11. Pushbuttons

Four normally-open pushbuttons are used.

All are **active HIGH**.

Each button connects its GPIO to +3.3 V when pressed.

Each input has:

* External 10 kΩ pulldown to GND
* 100 nF capacitor from GPIO to GND

### GPIO Assignment

| GPIO   | Button     |
| ------ | ---------- |
| GPIO34 | Lift Close |
| GPIO35 | Lift Open  |
| GPIO18 | Door Close |
| GPIO22 | Door Open  |

# 12. Photosensor

The photosensor is an analog sensor.

### Connections

* Supply → +3.3 V
* Ground → GND
* Analog output → GPIO36

No 5 V level shifting is required.

The photosensor is **not powered through the BTS7960 logic-power switch**.

Input filtering/protection remains TBD pending selection of the exact photosensor.

# 13. Travel-Time Potentiometers

Two potentiometers provide adjustable travel-time settings.

Target:

**Bourns 3352E-1-105** or equivalent.

Requirements:

* 1 MΩ
* Single turn
* Through-hole
* Top-adjust
* 3 terminals

### Lift Pot

* End 1 → +3.3 V
* End 2 → GND
* Wiper → GPIO33
* 100 nF wiper → GND

### Door Pot

* End 1 → +3.3 V
* End 2 → GND
* Wiper → GPIO39
* 100 nF wiper → GND

Firmware should allow settling time and use multiple ADC readings/averaging.

# 18. PCB Electrical Separation

Keep the following areas separated as much as practical:

### High-current / noisy wiring

* Battery motor wiring
* BTS7960 motor outputs
* Actuator wiring

These remain off-board.

### Sensitive circuitry

* ESP32
* Battery ADC
* Photosensor ADC
* Travel potentiometer ADCs
* Pushbutton inputs
* Limit-switch inputs

Keep sensitive analog traces away from motor-controller wiring and switching nodes.
